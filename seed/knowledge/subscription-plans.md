# Subscription Plans

Aurora Home offers two plans: **Free** and **Plus**. Every thermostat works on the Free
plan with no subscription required; Plus adds advanced features for £3.99/month or
£39.99/year (a saving of just over 16% versus paying monthly).

## Free plan

Included at no cost with every registered Aurora Thermostat:

- Manual and scheduled heating control (up to 6 periods per day)
- Eco mode and geofencing
- Current + previous month's energy report (see `energy-reports.md`)
- Holiday mode
- Standard email support with a 2 business day response time

## Plus plan -- £3.99/month or £39.99/year

Everything in Free, plus:

- Multi-zone scheduling for households with more than one thermostat
- Unlimited energy report history and CSV data export
- Neighbourhood comparison in your energy reports
- Priority support with a same-day response time
- Early access to new features before they roll out to Free plan users

## Changing or cancelling your plan

You can upgrade to Plus, downgrade to Free, or cancel at any time from **Settings >
Subscription** in the app. Downgrading takes effect at the end of your current billing
period -- you keep Plus features until then, and you are not charged again after
cancelling.

## Refunds

Plus subscription payments are non-refundable for partial billing periods, but if you
cancel within 14 days of your first Plus payment you can request a full refund of that
payment by contacting support. This is separate from the hardware returns policy -- see
`returns-and-warranty.md` for returning a physical thermostat.

## Family sharing

A single Plus subscription covers every Aurora Thermostat registered to your household in
the app, so you only need one subscription even if you have thermostats in multiple rooms.
