# Frequently Asked Questions

**Does Aurora Home work with smart home platforms?**
Yes. Aurora Thermostats work with Amazon Alexa and Google Home on both the Free and Plus
plans. Apple HomeKit support is planned but not yet available.

**Can I control the thermostat without an internet connection?**
Yes, for manual adjustments made directly on the thermostat's dial. Scheduled changes,
eco mode, and geofencing all require an internet connection since they're coordinated
through the Aurora Home app.

**How many thermostats can I pair to one account?**
There's no limit on the Free or Plus plan. Multi-zone independent scheduling for more than
one thermostat requires Plus (see `subscription-plans.md`).

**What HVAC systems are compatible?**
Most 24V systems with 2, 4, or 5 wires, including combi boilers with a standard room
thermostat wiring loop. Aurora Home is not compatible with 110V/120V mechanical systems
common in some older North American homes, millivolt systems, or systems without a
C-wire and no compatible adapter installed. Check compatibility with the wiring checker in
the Aurora Home app before ordering.

**Does Aurora Home sell smart plugs or other smart home devices?**
No. Aurora Home currently makes only the Aurora Thermostat. We don't manufacture smart
plugs, smart bulbs, security cameras, or any other product line, so support can't help with
devices from other manufacturers even if they're connected to the same smart home app
ecosystem.

**How do I contact a human if the chat assistant can't help?**
The in-app chat assistant can escalate to a human support agent at any point during a
conversation -- just ask to speak to a person. Registered users can also email
support@aurorahome.example (a placeholder address for this starter project) directly.

**Is the Aurora Thermostat compatible with underfloor heating?**
Yes, with the appropriate wiring adapter -- see the wiring checker in the app for
compatibility with your specific underfloor heating controller.
