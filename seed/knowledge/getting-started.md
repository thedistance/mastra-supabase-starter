# Getting Started with Aurora Home

Welcome to Aurora Home! This guide walks you through unboxing and pairing your Aurora
Thermostat for the first time.

## What's in the box

Every Aurora Thermostat ships with:

- The Aurora Thermostat display unit
- A wall mounting plate and two screws
- A wiring adapter compatible with most 24V HVAC systems
- A USB-C charging cable (the thermostat has a small backup battery)
- A quick-start card with a QR code

## Step 1: Charge before you install

Charge the thermostat for at least 30 minutes using the included USB-C cable before
mounting it. A full charge takes about 2 hours and powers the display for up to 3 days if
your HVAC wiring ever loses power.

## Step 2: Turn off power to your HVAC system

At your breaker panel, switch off the circuit that powers your furnace or air handler.
Aurora Home is not responsible for damage caused by wiring the thermostat with the power
still on.

## Step 3: Mount the wall plate

Use the included screws to mount the wall plate where your old thermostat was. The wiring
adapter supports standard 2-wire, 4-wire, and 5-wire (with a C-wire) HVAC setups.

## Step 4: Connect to Wi-Fi

Press and hold the dial for 3 seconds to enter setup mode, then scan the QR code on the
quick-start card with the Aurora Home app (iOS 16+ or Android 11+). The app will walk you
through joining your home Wi-Fi network. Aurora Thermostats only support 2.4GHz Wi-Fi
networks, not 5GHz.

## Step 5: Create your first schedule

Once connected, the app suggests a starter heating schedule based on your postcode and
typical UK weather patterns. You can accept it as-is or customize it -- see
`heating-schedules.md` for details.

## Registering your thermostat

Registering your thermostat inside the Aurora Home app activates your 2-year warranty
(see `returns-and-warranty.md`) and enables energy reports. Registration takes under a
minute and only requires an email address.

If you get stuck at any step, the in-app chat assistant (the one you're talking to now, if
you're using it through the starter) can help troubleshoot pairing and Wi-Fi issues.
