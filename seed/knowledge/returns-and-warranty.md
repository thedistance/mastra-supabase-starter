# Returns & Warranty

## 30-day returns

If you're not happy with your Aurora Thermostat, you can return it within 30 days of
delivery for a full refund. The thermostat must be in resaleable condition with all original
accessories (wall plate, screws, wiring adapter, and USB-C cable) included. Start a return
from **Settings > Order History > Return Item** in the app, or contact support with your
order number if you no longer have access to the app.

Refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5-10 business days of Aurora Home
receiving the returned item. Return shipping is free within the UK; a prepaid label is
generated when you start the return.

## 2-year warranty

Every Aurora Thermostat comes with a 2-year warranty from the date of registration (not the
date of purchase), covering manufacturing defects and hardware failures under normal
household use. Registering your thermostat in the app (see `getting-started.md`) activates
the warranty automatically.

The warranty covers:

- Display or touch-dial malfunctions
- Battery failures
- Wi-Fi radio failures
- Sensor inaccuracies beyond ±1°C from a calibrated reference

The warranty does not cover:

- Damage from incorrect wiring or installation by an unqualified installer
- Physical damage from drops, water, or extreme temperatures
- Normal cosmetic wear

## Making a warranty claim

Contact support with your order number and a description of the fault. Most claims are
resolved by shipping a replacement unit once the fault is confirmed; you'll receive a
prepaid label to return the faulty unit within 14 days of receiving the replacement.

## After the warranty expires

Aurora Home offers discounted replacement units to customers whose thermostat develops a
hardware fault after the 2-year warranty has expired. Contact support to check current
pricing.
