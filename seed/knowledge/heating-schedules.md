# Heating Schedules

Aurora Thermostats let you set up to 6 temperature changes per day, with different
schedules for weekdays and weekends.

## Creating a schedule

In the Aurora Home app, go to **Schedule** and tap **+ Add Period**. Each period has a
start time and a target temperature. A typical UK household schedule looks like:

- 06:30 -- 08:00: 20°C (morning warm-up)
- 08:00 -- 17:00: 16°C (away / eco)
- 17:00 -- 22:30: 20°C (evening)
- 22:30 -- 06:30: 15°C (night / sleep)

## Eco mode

Eco mode automatically lowers your target temperature by 3°C during periods marked "away"
if the Aurora Home app detects (via your phone's location, with permission) that no
registered household member is home. Eco mode is included on both the Free and Plus plans
-- see `subscription-plans.md` for the full feature comparison.

## Holiday mode

Tap **Holiday Mode** on the home screen to hold your thermostat at a fixed frost-protection
temperature of 7°C until a date you choose, then automatically resume your normal schedule.

## Manual overrides

Turning the dial to set a temperature manually creates a temporary override that lasts
until the next scheduled period change, at which point the schedule resumes automatically.
To hold a manual temperature indefinitely, tap **Hold** on the display after adjusting it.

## Multi-zone scheduling (Plus only)

Households with more than one Aurora Thermostat on the Plus plan can group thermostats into
zones (for example "Upstairs" and "Downstairs") and schedule each zone independently. Free
plan accounts can pair multiple thermostats but all of them share a single schedule.

## Geofencing

Geofencing complements eco mode by triggering an immediate switch to your "away" or "home"
temperature target as soon as your phone crosses a boundary you set around your house,
rather than waiting for the next scheduled period. Geofencing requires location permission
for the Aurora Home app and is available on both plans.
