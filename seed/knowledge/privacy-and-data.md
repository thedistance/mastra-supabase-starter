# Privacy & Data

Aurora Home collects the minimum data needed to control your heating and generate useful
insights.

## What we collect

- **Temperature and humidity readings** from your thermostat's onboard sensor, recorded at
  5-minute intervals while the thermostat is online.
- **Heating on/off events**, used to calculate runtime for energy reports.
- **Approximate location**, only if you enable geofencing, used solely to detect when you
  cross a boundary you've set. Aurora Home does not store a continuous location history --
  only the most recent "inside" or "outside" boundary state.
- **Account details**: email address and, if provided, a display name.

## What we don't collect

Aurora Home does not use a camera or microphone (the thermostat doesn't have either), does
not sell data to third parties, and does not use your heating data to target advertising.

## Data retention

Runtime and temperature data is retained for 24 months to power historical energy reports
(Plus plan) and neighbourhood comparisons, then automatically deleted. Account details are
retained until you delete your account.

## Anonymous accounts

You can use most Aurora Home features, including the in-app support chat, without creating
a registered account. Anonymous accounts are not linked to an email address and cannot
receive warranty coverage or order history, since both require a way to contact you. You can
link an anonymous account to a registered one at any time from **Settings > Account** without
losing your existing schedule or history.

## Deleting your data

Go to **Settings > Account > Delete Account** to permanently delete your account and all
associated data within 30 days. Deleting your account does not cancel an active Plus
subscription automatically -- cancel it separately first (see `subscription-plans.md`) to
avoid being charged again.

## Data location

All Aurora Home customer data is stored on servers located in the UK and EU.
