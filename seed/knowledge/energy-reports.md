# Energy Reports

Aurora Home turns your thermostat's runtime data into a monthly energy report, available in
the app under **Insights**.

## What's in a report

Each monthly report includes:

- Total heating hours for the month, compared to the previous month
- An estimated cost breakdown based on the energy tariff you enter in **Settings > Tariff**
- A comparison against similar-sized homes in your postcode area (Plus plan only)
- Suggestions for schedule changes that could reduce runtime, such as lowering your away
  temperature by 1-2°C

## Entering your tariff

Reports are only as accurate as the tariff you provide. Go to **Settings > Tariff** and
enter your pence-per-kWh rate and standing charge from your energy bill. Aurora Home does
not connect to your energy supplier automatically -- this must be entered manually.

## Report history

Free plan accounts can view their current month and the previous month's report. Plus plan
accounts get unlimited report history, going back to when the thermostat was first
registered.

## Exporting data

Plus plan subscribers can export raw runtime data as a CSV file from **Insights > Export**.
This includes timestamped heating on/off events and measured room temperature at 5-minute
intervals. Free plan accounts can view but not export this data.

## Why my estimated cost might be wrong

The most common causes of an inaccurate cost estimate are an out-of-date tariff, a home with
supplemental heating (space heaters, wood burners) that Aurora Home can't measure, or a
recent energy price change that hasn't been updated in **Settings > Tariff** yet. Aurora
Home estimates are for guidance only and are not a substitute for your actual energy bill.
